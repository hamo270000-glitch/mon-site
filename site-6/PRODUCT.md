# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS 4, GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText), Lenis, Three.js via @react-three/fiber and @react-three/drei. Built into `site-6/` and published by GitHub Pages. Same base as Site 4, different brand.

## Users
French owners of ageing dogs (grey muzzle, stiffness, arthrosis, dysplasia), mostly on a phone from social ads. They see their dog get up slowly, seek the cold tiled floor, change sides at night. Buyer is the owner; the beneficiary is the dog.

## Product Purpose
"Museau Gris" sells one orthopaedic bed, "Le Doyen". Success: the visitor understands in seconds why memory foam relieves pressure points, picks the right size and adds it to the cart.

## Positioning
95 € to 159 €. Earned through honesty and clarity (pressure points, layers, size), never through urgency.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Museau Gris" (chosen by the owner from three proposals). Style "Veillée du soir": night blue, amber lamp as the only vivid colour, Fraunces serif + Instrument Sans. Must stay visually distinct from Lové (Site 4: cream, terracotta, grotesque). French only, tutoiement, gentle and honest tone.

## Evidence on Hand
No customer reviews, no vet endorsement, no certification, no sales figures. Do not invent any. Specs are provisional and labelled as such. Never claim the bed treats arthrosis.

## Product Principles
1. The old dog's rest is the hero.
2. Explain pressure honestly; the x-ray scene is an illustration, said so on screen.
3. Size choice must be easy: it drives returns.
4. Calm beats urgency: no countdowns, no fake stock.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Respect prefers-reduced-motion (no scroll pinning, x-ray scene becomes a two-button comparison), small phones, 4.5:1 text contrast.
