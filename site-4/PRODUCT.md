# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS 4, GSAP (ScrollTrigger), Lenis, React Bits, Three.js via @react-three/fiber and @react-three/drei. Built into `site-4/` and published by GitHub Pages. Choice delegated by the owner, who asked for a 3D shop.

## Users
French dog owners, mostly on a phone (Instagram/TikTok ad traffic), whose dog is ageing, heavy, recently operated, or shows joint stiffness (arthrose, dysplasie). They want their dog to sleep without pain and to stop sleeping on the cold floor. Buyer is the owner; the beneficiary is the dog.

## Product Purpose
"Lové" is a dropshipping shop selling orthopaedic dog beds in France. Success: a visitor understands in seconds why a memory-foam bed helps joints, picks the right size, and adds it to the cart.

## Positioning
Price range 89 € to 149 € (premium for dropshipping). The shop has to earn that price through clarity about the foam layers, the right size for the dog, and a calm, trustworthy brand — not through discount pressure.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from social ads, browse on mobile, compare sizes against their dog's length and weight, and need delivery and return reassurance before paying.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pages: home, product page with 3D configurator (size, colour), "les couches" (how the bed is built), size guide, cart.
- Checkout is not connected yet (no Shopify yet): the cart works locally; the payment step is a placeholder.
- Undecided: real supplier, exact foam density and thickness, delivery times, final product photos.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Lové" (chosen by Claude, may change). French language only. Tone: gentle, reassuring, precise.

## Evidence on Hand
No real customer reviews, no vet endorsement, no certifications, no sales figures yet. Do not invent any of them. Product specs shown on the site are provisional and must be labelled as such until the supplier sample is validated.

## Product Principles
1. The dog's comfort is the hero, not the shop.
2. Explain the foam honestly; no fake medical claims.
3. Size choice must be easy: it drives returns.
4. Calm beats urgency: no countdowns, no fake stock.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Must respect prefers-reduced-motion (3D and scroll animation calm down), work on small phones, keep text contrast at 4.5:1.
